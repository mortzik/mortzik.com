01 About

3 : 4 : 5 : 7

Mortzik is an Iranian electronic music producer and soundtrack composer with a background in electronics engineering.

His work explores the intersection of sound and perception, drawing inspiration from synesthesia to craft intricate polyrhythms and immersive soundscapes. Through his compositions, Mortzik challenges listeners to explore new dimensions of music, pushing the boundaries of genre and emotion.

Discover his sonic world and join him on this artistic journey.
