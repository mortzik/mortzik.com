# mortzik.com

Personal website of Mortzik: electronic music producer, soundtrack composer and audio-visual artist.
Hosted on GitHub Pages at https://mortzik.com and built automatically by Jekyll.

## Editing the site (no code)

1. Go to https://app.pagescms.org and sign in with GitHub.
2. Open the `mortzik/mortzik.com` repository.
3. Edit **Releases**, **Works**, **Gallery** or **Bio, links & contact**, then click **Save**.
   The live site updates about a minute later.

## Where things live

- `_data/site.yml`: intro line, bio, portrait, email, streaming and social links
- `_data/releases.yml`: discography (newest first; the first or "featured" one shows at the top)
- `_data/works.yml`: audio-visual and sound design projects
- `_data/gallery.yml`: photo groups
- `images/`: originals; `images/web/` and `images/thumbs/` are smaller copies for speed
- `index.html`: page template; `style.css`: styling; `script.js`: animated background and lightbox
- `favicon.svg`, `assets/`: monogram logo (#0E0E10 on #F2F0EA), app icons, social preview image
- `.pages.yml`: configuration for the editing panel
